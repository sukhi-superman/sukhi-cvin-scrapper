BOM Compliance Checker

Upload a CycloneDX or SPDX SBOM (JSON), or an IntelliXBOM machine export. The file is read in your browser. It is checked against CERT-In's 21 SBOM fields, CVEs with CERT-In vulnerability notes, the EU Cyber Resilience Act and NIST guidance.

Sends package IDs and CVE IDs (never the file) to OSV, deps.dev, ecosyste.ms, endoflife.date, FIRST EPSS and the CERT-In notes index.
Frameworks: CERT-In Technical Guidelines on SBOM, QBOM & CBOM, AIBOM & HBOM v2.0 (21 SBOM fields) · EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) Annex I, read with BSI TR-03183-2 · NTIA minimum elements as referenced by NIST for EO 14028, NIST SP 800-218, SP 800-40r4, IR 8547, SP 800-53r5. CERT-In notes come from the unofficial Sukhi CVIN index (sukhi-superman.github.io/sukhi-cvin-scrapper/v2/), which mirrors cert-in.org.in. This tool gives an indication, not a legal compliance opinion.
